What we do

Four services. Two trades. One crew.

Renovations & Construction

Kitchens, bathrooms, basements and full interior renovations — framing through finishing carpentry. Built properly, and handed back clean.

Post-Construction Cleaning

The deep turnover clean after the trades leave: drywall dust, paint spatter, adhesive residue, window and fixture detail. Available on jobs we didn't build.

Residential Cleaning

Recurring housekeeping, seasonal deep cleans, and move-in / move-out turnovers that actually pass an inspection.

Commercial Cleaning

Offices, retail units and strata common areas, scheduled around your hours instead of ours.

Questions

Good to know.

Do you really do both construction and cleaning?

Yes — that's the whole idea, and it's the name on the door. Most clients hire us for a renovation and the final clean comes with it. You can also book either one on its own.

Can I book cleaning without a renovation?

Absolutely. Recurring residential cleaning, one-off deep cleans, move-in and move-out turnovers, and commercial contracts are all standalone services.

Do you do post-construction cleanup for other contractors?

Yes. Builders and renovators hire us for turnover cleans on projects we had no part in building. Get in touch with the site details and timeline.

What areas do you serve?

Drayton Valley and the surrounding Brazeau County communities — Breton, Lodgepole, Cynthia, Buck Creek, Rocky Rapids and Warburg. If you're further out, ask — we may still be able to help.

How do I get a quote?

Call or email and we'll arrange a walkthrough. For renovations we quote the build and the final clean together, so you get one number rather than two.
